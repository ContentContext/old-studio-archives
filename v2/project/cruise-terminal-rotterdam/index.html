It’s not always sunny in Rotterdam.

In collaboration with Rob Tak Artistic Productions
http://www.cruiseportrotterdam.com
http://www.robtak.nl

Cruise Port Rotterdam, 2013

The Cruise Terminal served as the arrival and departure point of the Holland America Line and is part of the industrial heritage. It is still used by large passenger vessels that visit Rotterdam. The Terminal is now known for its cafe/restaurant located inside with a hall of 3800 m².

This way finding signage for the departing and arriving passengers at the Cruise Terminal in Rotterdam. The signage was printed on banners that could be applied on a daily base. The typeface was created especially for the Cruise Port of Rotterdam.

Rotterdam isn’t always the sunniest of places. We wanted to give the disembarking travelers a soft, warm and welcoming first impression.
