Bruegel is a Brussels based European think tank specializing in economics. Their new publication EU 2 Do is a collection of scholarly essays to improve the quality of economic policy with open and fact-based research, analysis and debate.

We worked closely with the editorial team at Bruegel to shift their current design course to a fresher one. With a little bit of analysis and trust they let us do our thing.

When the publication was finally released it was received with a smashing success. It’s now out
of print.

We also made a digital version for easier distribution and access for the poor
folks who couldn’t get their hands on a physical copy.
