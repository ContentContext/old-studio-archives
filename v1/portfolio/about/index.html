Before this Michael started his design career at Winterhouse Studio in Fallsvillage, Connecticut under the direction of William Drenttel and Jessica Helfand, 2004 – 2005. From 2005 – 2007 he worked as a designer with Tsang Seymour Design in New York City then from 2007 – 2009 he was senior designer at MGMT. Design in Brooklyn, New York. He then left for Rotterdam the Netherlands where he founded VosBrenner Design 2009-2013.

He also taught at the Willem de Kooning Academy in Rotterdam in both the Graphic Design and Lifestyle departments from 2011-2015.

We practice design with a holistic approach that is research driven, which aims to clarify, surprise, engage and inform to generate understanding. Above all design is a question of interaction and relationships.

We are always searching for new uncharted territories to bring our expertise to.

Working with Content Context is about people working together to connect the dots.

HOW WE GET THINGS DONE:

Each job has its own content and context.
We research, study and ask a lot of questions before we start.

First we explore in search of the improbable.
Then we design.

If we don’t know, we ask.
Not everyone is a rocket scientist.

We are not afraid to make mistakes.
We learn from them and use them as a departure point.

Everything created is a tool for communication.
We design for people.
