How do you brand a mythical territory?FreedomLandIdea, Identity, Places, Research, Transparency

This exhibition was held at Casla in Almere.
http://www.casla.nl/

How and where do you begin by branding a fictional territory that has been made visible through photography? We were completely vexed by this notion at first also given the deep and rich history of the project.

You start with a flag!

Freedomland is a visual, journalistic statement, into an highly regulated- man made landscape; captured between forces of order and space, landscape and urbanism, concept and execution, authority and freedom and expressed as a new conceptual territory. This surreal setting can only be viewed through the lens and presented to us by photographer Denis Guzzo.

The gallery space was problematic for a show of this size. So we hung the photos backwards, when viewers entered the space they were confronted with the backsides of the photos first. The director of the space was skeptical at first but loved it in the end.
