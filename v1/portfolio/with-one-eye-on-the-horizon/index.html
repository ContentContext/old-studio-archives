How to reinvent moving images with one eye on the horizon.Identity, Places, Research

TENT. Gallery, Rotterdam, The Netherlands
Photography: Aad Hoogendoorn
http://pzwart.wdka.nl/home

With One Eye on the Horizon is the final presentation of the first graduates of the Master Media Design & Communication: Lens-Based at the Piet Zwart Institute, a postgraduate program at the Willem de Kooning Academy.

The Lens-Based program explores the possibilities of new forms (media) and platforms – streaming media, installations in galleries, movies on mobile phones, cross-media story lines, movie database technologies, site-specific projects and other hybrid forms. The program emphasizes new possibilities offered by the combination of still and moving images in a shared digital environment.
