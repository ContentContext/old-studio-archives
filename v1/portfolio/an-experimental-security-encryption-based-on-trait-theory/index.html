An experimental security encryption based on Trait Theory.(HEEM) HANS EYSENCK ENCRYPTION MODELIdea, Transparency

HEEM is an experimental security encryption based on Trait Theory, defined as the measurement of traits, which can be defined as habitual patters of behavior, thought and emotion.

According to this perspective, traits are relatively stable over time, differ among individuals (e.g. some people are outgoing whereas others are shy), and influence behavior.

These measurements are applied to HEEM and act as a sort of key generator to identify the authorized user.

An emotionally based security key is more effective in identifying an individuals than traditional methods. Think of this as the DNA of a personality.
