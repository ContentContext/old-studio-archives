The environment is important. People need to be clued in.DCMR Milieudienst RijnmondResearch, Stories, Transparency, Voices

We contacted the DCMR in hopes to get our hands on their content. We managed to meet with the DCMR several times to discuss plans on how to move forward with a new design for their publications. Framed as a test case we worked out several possibilities for their future publications. These tests we then exported into templates accompanied with style guides and instruction for their in house team.

Despite the fact that they were pleased with the results of our test case due to budgetary cuts they couldn’t take on our vision for them.
